Ge MRV Engine / Project Overview

CleanTech: Predictive Green Energy MRV Engine

An intelligent energy management platform designed to measure renewable energy usage, understand energy problems, predict future conditions, and support better energy-management decisions.

01 / BUILD Project Foundation
⚡

What We Are Building

CleanTech: Predictive Green Energy MRV Engine is an AI-powered web-based platform designed to monitor, analyze, and manage renewable energy usage in industrial environments.

The system collects time-based energy information such as solar generation, wind generation, electricity consumption, grid usage, battery status, and other relevant readings.

This data is processed to understand the current energy situation. The system calculates renewable energy generation, renewable energy matching, energy deficit, and grid dependency.

Machine learning is then used to learn from historical energy patterns and forecast future electricity demand and renewable generation for the next 24–48 hours.

Core idea: CleanTech does not simply display energy data. It understands the current situation, predicts future conditions, and helps the operator decide what action should be taken.

02 / PROBLEM Why It Is Needed
⚠

Why This Project Is Needed

Renewable energy sources such as solar and wind are intermittent. Their generation changes according to environmental conditions, while industrial electricity demand can continue throughout the day.

This creates a mismatch between the time when clean energy is generated and the time when electricity is actually consumed.

Energy Imbalance: Renewable generation can be high when demand is low and low when demand is high.

Renewable Mismatch: Available solar or wind energy may not match the exact period of electricity consumption.

Slow Verification: Manual or aggregate reporting can make it difficult to understand what happened during individual time intervals.

No Future Visibility: Operators need to know about possible future energy shortages before they occur.

Battery Monitoring: Energy storage systems need monitoring of charge level and operating conditions.

The actual challenge: How can an organization know whether renewable energy was available when energy was consumed, predict what will happen next, and take action before an energy deficit occurs?

03 / SOLUTION How CleanTech Solves It
✓

Our Solution

CleanTech combines energy monitoring, Measurement, Reporting & Verification (MRV), machine learning forecasting, and intelligent recommendations into one integrated platform.

Instead of only showing historical energy data, the system continuously analyzes energy conditions and uses those results to support future decisions.

01 Measure

Collect renewable generation, consumption, grid, and battery data at regular intervals.

02 Verify

Calculate renewable matching, energy deficit, and other MRV performance metrics.

03 Predict

Use machine learning to forecast future demand and renewable energy generation.

04 Recommend

Suggest better periods for flexible loads when renewable energy availability is favorable.

05 Monitor

Track battery State of Charge, temperature, alerts, and energy performance.

06 Visualize

Present KPIs, charts, forecasts, alerts, and recommendations through a dashboard.

Collect Data → Process → Measure & Verify → Predict → Recommend → Dashboard

Final goal: Help organizations understand their renewable energy performance, identify potential energy deficits early, and make better decisions for using clean energy efficiently.
