What we are building

Predictive Green Energy MRV Engine : A scalable infrastructure-as-a-service platform designed to automate verifiable net-zero compliance for the next generation of industrial energy grids.
Renewable energy (such as solar and wind) is intermittent, whereas sectors like EV charging and data centers have massive, continuous energy demands. Currently, the methods for tracking how "green" (environmentally friendly) the consumed energy is are slow and manual, making it difficult to meet net-zero goals.
That's why we need, an advanced Measurement, Reporting, and Verification (MRV) engine that functions as an intelligent digital auditor and automated controller.

The Problems

  • Energy Imbalance: Renewable energy generation (such as solar and wind) is not continuous—it is intermittent—whereas energy demand from sources like EV fleets or data centers remains constant around the clock.
  • Outdated and Slow Tracking: Current methods for auditing green energy are slow, manual, and based on antiquated 'annual matching' systems.
  • Lack of Transparency: This absence of real-time control and transparency jeopardizes global net-zero commitments and compliance with regulations.

The Solutions

How will this project change the game?
This new MRV Engine eliminates the need for year-long waits. By replacing outdated tracking methods, it establishes an automated verification trail that tracks data in real-time at 15-minute intervals.
The system performs a check every 15 minutes:
  • did wind or solar energy generation actually occur during the exact 15-minute window when the company consumed 10 units of electricity?

  • If not, the 'Carbon Deficit Heatmap' will immediately indicate that there was a shortfall of clean energy at that time.

    • Main Solutions-
    • Real-time Auditing: Replacing traditional manual methods, this system generates an independent digital verification trail at strict 15-minute intervals.
    • Predictive Intelligence: Utilizing machine learning, it forecasts spikes in energy demand and renewable generation 24 to 48 hours in advance.
    • Proactive Optimization: The system suggests load-shifting strategies to reduce reliance on polluting grid backup.
    • Asset Protection: It tracks the health of battery storage through continuous telemetry monitoring and protects against thermal degradation.
